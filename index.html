00 // Overview

707 acres of engineering, drawn to the scale of a country.

The National University of Sciences & Technology is where Pakistan trains its engineers, architects, scientists and founders — on one campus at the foot of the Margalla Hills, with a technology park built into its southern edge.

02 // Admissions

The NET roadmap.

Four stages from eligibility to a hostel key. Open each stage for the detail.

Merit = Σ weighted inputs

NET 75% HSSC 15% SSC 10%

Engineering & computing programmes. Weightings vary for some disciplines — confirm on the official admissions portal.

Progress
Stage 01 Eligibility

Check that you meet the academic requirement for your intended discipline before registering.

  • Minimum 60% marks in SSC and HSSC (or equivalent) for engineering & computing
  • Pre-Engineering / ICS for engineering; Pre-Medical routes to biosciences
  • A-Level students apply with IBCC equivalence
  • Overseas & dual-nationals may apply through SAT scores
Stage 02 NET Registration

The NUST Entry Test runs as a computer-based test in several series through the year. You may sit more than one series; your best score counts.

Indicative series schedule — cycle 2027
Series Window Mode
NET-1 Dec – Jan Computer-based
NET-2 Feb – Mar Computer-based
NET-3 Apr – May Computer-based
NET-4 Jun – Jul Computer-based
Stage 03 Merit Lists

Aggregates combine NET, HSSC and SSC. Merit lists are published in rounds; your programme preference order decides where you are placed.

  • Rank your programme & campus preferences carefully — they're binding per round
  • Pay the admission dues by the deadline on your offer to secure the seat
  • Later lists move as candidates upgrade or withdraw
Stage 04 Hostel Allocation

Separate boys' and girls' hostels sit inside the H-12 perimeter. Allocation follows merit and distance from home, subject to availability.

  • Apply for accommodation once your admission is confirmed
  • Priority to students from outside Islamabad / Rawalpindi
  • Mess, laundry and the Concordia food courts within walking distance

All dates & criteria indicative. The official admissions portal is authoritative.

03 // Research & Industry

NSTP

A technology park inside the campus fence.

The National Science & Technology Park puts companies, incubated startups and research labs on the same ground as the lecture halls. A final-year project can begin in a SEECS lab, find a sponsor in an NSTP tower, and spin out from the incubator — without leaving H-12.

“Universities teach. Parks build. The interesting part is the walk between them.”

— Editor's note, Sheet 03

Societies

Debating, science, dramatics, photography, ACM and IEEE chapters — recruitment week fills the plaza every autumn.

Floodlit grounds, a sports complex and the inter-school NUST Olympiad, contested more fiercely than most finals.

Shuttle loops connect the schools, hostels and NSTP. Most students walk; the Margallas stay in view the whole way.
