Coffee

Coffee is a famous drink in the world.
Enjoy a fresh cup every day !!!

Today's tip: Fresh beans taste better than old ones. Try to use them within 2 weeks of roasting.

How to brew a cup of coffee

  1. Boil fresh water
  2. Grind your coffee beans
  3. Add the coffee to your filter
  4. Pour the hot water slowly
  5. Wait, then enjoy

Coffee facts

  • 1 Coffee beans are actually the seeds of a fruit called a coffee cherry.
  • 2 Finland drinks the most coffee per person in the world.
  • 3 Espresso actually has less caffeine per cup than regular filter coffee.
  • 4 The word "coffee" comes from the Arabic word qahwa.
