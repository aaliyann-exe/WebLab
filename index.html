Our Story

Started in a rented workshop.

Verran began in 2014, when Claire Verran left a large fragrance house and took a lease on a room above a bakery in Grasse. She wanted to make perfumes she could afford to wear herself, with fewer ingredients and more of the good ones.

We still work in small lots. Every batch rests for ten weeks, is tested by three people who were not involved in blending it, and is bottled by hand. If a batch is off, we pour it out.

— Claire Verran, founder
