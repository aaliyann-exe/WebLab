A room above a bakery

“I left a big perfume house because I was told to make things that sell. I wanted to make things I could not stop smelling.”

In 2014 I rented one room above a bakery in Grasse. The ovens warmed the floor, and for the first winter every sample I made smelled faintly of bread. I kept the room for six years. I still buy my bergamot from the same grower, and I still weigh every batch myself.

We make 400 bottles at a time and let them rest for ten weeks. Three people who didn’t blend the batch smell it blind, and all three have to approve it. If they don’t, I pour it out and start again. It has happened four times, and I would do it again tomorrow.

Claire Verran

Founder and perfumer
